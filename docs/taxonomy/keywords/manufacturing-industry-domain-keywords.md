# Harvester Manufacturing and Industry Domain Keyword Registry

Status: **Proposed**  
Version: **1.2**  
Date: **5 August 2026**  
Implementation status: **Not implemented**

The complete **111-domain** Manufacturing and Industry keyword registry is split into two reviewable files:

- [Part 1](manufacturing-industry-domain-keywords-part-1.md)
- [Part 2](manufacturing-industry-domain-keywords-part-2.md)

The parts preserve separate keyword profiles for every canonical domain. Activity keywords continue to resolve through the canonical activity registry and existing domain–activity mapping. Service-gap and buying-intent terms remain provider-service-specific.

Keywords identify evidence candidates only. They do not independently prove domain membership, business activity, service need, buying intent, a service gap, or lead qualification.
